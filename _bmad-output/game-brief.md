## Game Vision

### Core Concept

Tower Oblivion is a story-driven heroic-fantasy roguelite investigation game where an amnesiac prisoner climbs a deadly tower, keeping skills and discovered memories across runs to uncover the truth and escape.

### Elevator Pitch

You awaken in the basement of a mysterious tower with no memory of how you got there. Each run pushes you through new floors filled with tougher enemies, clues, and procedural encounters in a photo-novel / point-and-click presentation where combat and interactions create meaningful choices. Death resets your equipment but not your growth: your abilities, knowledge, and past decisions persist, opening new paths in future runs until the final revelation.

### Vision Statement

Tower Oblivion aims to deliver an addictive "one more run" loop where narrative discovery is as rewarding as progression, and where player choices can sacrifice short-term gain for long-term impact across runs. The game's identity is not technical spectacle but a coherent mystery, strong worldbuilding, and deep meta-progression that makes every attempt meaningful. The MVP will prioritize a modular gameplay engine (runs, progression, memory system, inventory, event resolution) so content, combat innovation, and story branches can be expanded cleanly over time.
